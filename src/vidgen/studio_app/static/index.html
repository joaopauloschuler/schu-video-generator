      Space

      pin a listening note at the moment playing
      L
      loop the section playing; again: the whole piece

      mute / solo the selected track (what plays, not the score)

      Welcome to schu-video-generator studio

      1. Pick a preset (a built-in bed) and press Space to listen.
      2. Change one thing: a track's pattern, a slider, the tempo. Each change is a new version, rendered by schu-video-generator.
      3. Read the Check at the bottom, then A/B it against the original.
      4. Pin notes with N, Ask an agent, try Variations, then Export.

            Ask an agent

            Hands the score, the open notes, the check and the last change to an AI agent. It answers with a new version and one line per note on what it changed and why; you A/B it, keep what you like, add notes, repeat.
