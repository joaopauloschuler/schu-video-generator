# Card: The Rainbow Ride

# Scene: orchard
set: background: day; props: tree at 0.28, gift at 0.46, watering_can at 0.4, bicycle at 0.78; characters: tibbo at 0.35, fern at 0.87

Every morning, Tibbo the robot watered the old apple tree.

And every morning, the old apple tree said thank you.

TIBBO (dazed): [beep] Three apples. No. One apple. Spinning.

Then Fern came down the lane on her bicycle.

FERN (giggling): [laugh] Tibbo, you look like a spinning top! Hurry up. It is Nana's birthday!

TIBBO (happy): The present! I kept it safe all week.

Just then, dark clouds rolled over the orchard.

TIBBO (scared): Thunder! All my bolts are rattling!

FERN (happy): It is only a little shower. Look, a rainbow! It ends right over Nana's house.

[Tibbo exits right.]

So off they went to the party, one pedalling and one rolling.

# Scene: cottage
set: room: dots, boards, window at 0.45, door at 0.13; props: clock at 0.8 on the wall, fireplace at 0.8 against the wall, table at 0.25, cake at 0.22, kettle at 0.28, armchair at 0.63, lamp at 0.94, gift at 0.13; characters: nana at 0.63, fern at 0.36, tibbo at 0.51

At Nana Pell's cottage, the fire was crackling and the kettle was singing.

NANA (she, happy): Come in, my dears, come in! Mind the cake.

TIBBO: Happy birthday, Nana! We brought you something. [beep]

NANA (embarrassed): A present for me? Oh, you two. You really should not have.

FERN (teasing): Tibbo chose it all alone. Only one apple was harmed.

So they ate cake by the fire, while the evening grew dark outside.
