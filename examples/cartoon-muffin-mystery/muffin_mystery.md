On Saturday evening, Mira baked a basket of blueberry muffins.

MIRA (she, happy): These are for the picnic tomorrow. Nobody eats one tonight. Not even you, Crumb.

[Crumb falls in love with the basket.]

CRUMB (he): Who, me? I am a very good dog.

So the whole house went to bed, and the muffins waited on the counter, all alone.

[Crumb falls asleep.]

In the morning, Olen came down for breakfast, still half asleep.

[Olen enters from the left.]

MIRA (shocked): Olen! The muffins are gone! The whole basket!

OLEN (he, proud): Leave this to me. I am a detective.

[Olen runs to the counter.]

OLEN (thinking): An empty counter. Crumbs on the floor. And one dog who looks very guilty.

[Crumb sweats.]

CRUMB (nervous): I was asleep! I only dreamed about muffins!

[Crumb jumps.]

MIRA: Olen. What is that in your hand?

[Olen looks at the camera.]

OLEN: Well. Case closed.

Crumb got the first muffin anyway.
