# Rain Check

# Scene: meadow
set: background: day; props: tree at 0.9, basket at 0.3, cloud at 0.04; characters: pip at 0.22, juno at 0.68

It was a perfect morning for a picnic, and Pip had packed for two.

PIP (she, happy): I packed sandwiches, napkins and lemonade. Will the weather hold, Juno?

JUNO (he, proud): Not a cloud in the sky, Pip. I checked.

[A small grey cloud drifts in behind Juno.]

The cloud had not checked.

# Scene: picnic
set: background: day; props: tree at 0.9, rock at 0.38, rock (stone) at 0.6, cloud at 0.7; characters: pip at 0.3, juno at 0.68

[Pip and Juno sit on the two flat rocks. Pip holds a sandwich wrapped in a napkin.]

PIP: One cheese sandwich, in a napkin, for our weather expert.

[Pip gives the napkin to Juno.]

JUNO (happy): Thank you. A perfect day for a perfect sandwich.

[The wind picks up. The tree sways, and the napkin blows out of Juno's hand.]

JUNO: Ah. That is a refreshing breeze.

[Close-up on Pip.]

PIP (deadpan): That was your sandwich, Juno.

# Scene: storm
set: background: day; props: tree at 0.9, rock at 0.38, rock (stone) at 0.6, basket at 0.46; characters: pip at 0.3, juno at 0.68

[It starts to rain. The light goes grey.]

Then the cloud did what clouds do.

PIP (shocked): Rain! Grab the basket and run for the tree!

[Pip runs to the tree. Juno runs to the tree.]

JUNO: Relax, Pip! It is just a little shower!

PIP (cross): It is a storm, Juno! A real storm!

JUNO: Trust me, a shower like this never lasts longer than a minute.

PIP: Juno. Your umbrella.

[Juno opens his umbrella. A gust takes it.]

The umbrella left without him.

# Scene: night
set: background: night; props: tree at 0.9, basket at 0.76; characters: pip at 0.7, juno at 0.84

By nightfall, the rain had finally run out of rain.

PIP (sad): Our sandwiches are soup, Juno.

JUNO (proud): Tomorrow will be sunny. I checked.

It rained for the rest of the week.
