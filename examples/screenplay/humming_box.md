# The Humming Box

# Scene: yard
set: background: day; props: house at 0.18, box (crate) at 0.6, tree at 0.84; characters: fen at left, bram at right

On a quiet Sunday, Fen heard a sound that did not belong in the yard.

FEN (surprised): Bram, listen! That box is humming a song.

[Fen walks to the crate and points at it.]

BRAM: Boxes do not hum, Fen. Boxes do not do *anything*.

FEN: Then who is singing? Not me, and not you.

[Bram walks to the crate. He looks at Fen.]

BRAM (worried): Fine. It is humming. I do not like it at all.

Somewhere inside, the song went up a whole note.

# Scene: reveal
set: background: day; props: house at 0.18, box (crate) at 0.6, tree at 0.84; characters: fen at left, bram at 0.45

[Gus enters from the right.]

GUS (glad): There it is! I left my song in that crate this morning.

BRAM (arms crossed): You keep songs in boxes? Is that normal?

GUS: Only the loud ones. The quiet ones live in my hat.

[Fen and Bram look at each other.]

FEN (happy): Can we keep a song in our crate too? Please?

[Gus nods.]

And that is how the yard got its very first song box.
